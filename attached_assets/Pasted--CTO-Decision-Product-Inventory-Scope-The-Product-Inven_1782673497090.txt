# CTO Decision — Product Inventory Scope

The Product Inventory module shall follow **Option A** with the following clarification.

Product Inventory is **not** a separate inventory engine.

It is a **read-only operational view** over the frozen Product Platform.

The Product Platform remains the single source of truth.

No new inventory tables.

No new stock ledger.

No warehouse redesign.

No movement engine.

No duplicate product records.

## Product Inventory shall display

### Inventory Summary

* Total Products
* Available Products
* Packed Products
* Dispatched Products
* Dealer Stock
* Quarantined Products

---

### Filters

* Product Category
* Product Model
* Product Status
* Current Location
* Manufacturing Date
* Dealer
* Product Serial

---

### Product Categories

* Battery Pack
* Inbuilt Lithium Inverter
* Hybrid Inverter

---

### Product Status

Only use the existing Product Platform lifecycle.

Do not introduce additional inventory statuses.

---

### Product Detail

Each Product shall display:

* Official Product Serial
* Product Category
* Product Model
* Manufacturing Workflow
* Manufacturing Date
* Current Status
* Current Location
* Product Genealogy
* Product Event Timeline

Reuse the existing Product Platform pages wherever possible.

---

### Search

Support search by:

* Product Serial
* Model
* Dealer

---

### Dashboard

Provide summary cards for:

* Ready for Packing
* Packed
* Dispatched
* Dealer Inventory

These are projections from Product data only.

---

### Architecture Rule

The Product Platform remains the only owner of:

* Product Identity
* Product Lifecycle
* Product Events
* Product Genealogy

Product Inventory is only a presentation and reporting layer.

It shall never create, modify, or duplicate Product records.

No new architecture.

No new inventory engine.

Simply expose the capabilities already provided by the frozen Product Platform.

Proceed with implementation.
