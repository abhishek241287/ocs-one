My recommendation

Do not add any new UI components outside ODS from now on.

If someone needs a new button, dialog, table, or form pattern, add it to ODS first and then reuse it everywhere. That discipline will keep OCS One maintainable as it grows from 30 modules to 100+.

I honestly think this is one of the smartest architectural decisions you've made on the project so far. It will pay dividends for years.
