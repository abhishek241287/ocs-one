# OCS One – Sprint 3.1B

## Battery Workspace (Foundation)

You are the Lead Enterprise Software Engineer for OCS One.

We have completed:

* Authentication
* Dashboard
* Master Data Framework
* Production Orders
* Battery Creation

Your task is to build the **Battery Workspace**, which will become the permanent digital home for every battery throughout its lifecycle.

This page must be designed so future modules (Cell Allocation, Assembly, QC, Warranty, Service, AI) can plug into it without redesign.

---

# Route

/manufacturing/batteries/:batteryId
