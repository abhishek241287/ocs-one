# IMPORTANT ARCHITECTURAL CLARIFICATION

I do **not** want Product creation to be hardcoded to **QC PASS**.

Instead, Product creation shall be **workflow-driven**.

The workflow defines **when a Product becomes a finished Product**.

Examples:

### BATTERY

Product Creation Trigger

**QC PASS**

---

### INBUILT_LITHIUM

Product Creation Trigger

**QC PASS**

---

### HYBRID

Product Creation Trigger

**Incoming Inspection PASS**

There is no manufacturing assembly for Hybrid Inverters.

There is no battery genealogy.

The Product becomes available immediately after Incoming Inspection approval.

---

This approach keeps the Product Platform generic and prevents another architectural change when Hybrid Inverter inventory is implemented.

The Product Platform provides one unified Product identity.

The Manufacturing Workflow determines **when** that Product is created.

Product creation must therefore use the configured workflow trigger rather than a hardcoded QC PASS condition.

I recommend introducing a configurable Product Creation Trigger as part of the Workflow Master so future workflows can define their own completion event without changing Product Platform code.

Examples:

* BATTERY → QC_PASS
* INBUILT_LITHIUM → QC_PASS
* HYBRID → INCOMING_INSPECTION_PASS

This keeps the architecture extensible while remaining fully compatible with the frozen Product Platform v1.0.

# Design Principle

The Product Platform should become internally complete before anything external consumes it.

The objective is to build the Product Platform once, expose it once, and certify it once.

This avoids:

* API contract changes during CW-03
* repeated UI refactoring
* duplicated business logic
* future architectural redesign
* repeated certification effort

Please revise the remaining Phase 0 implementation sequence accordingly and continue implementation following this architecture.
