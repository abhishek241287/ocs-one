# OCS One – Sprint 3.1B

## Battery Workspace

You are the Lead Frontend and Backend Engineer for OCS One.

Use the existing Production Order and Battery modules.

Do NOT build Cell Allocation, Assembly, QC, Inventory, or Testing yet.
