I have only a few recommendations before you freeze this as the official Factory Ready v1.0 document.

1. Add Version Information (Very Important)

Right now it says:

Factory Ready v1.0

I would make it a formal release document.

At the top add:

Release Name:
Factory Ready v1.0

Release Date:
01 July 2026

Architecture Version:
Platform v1.0 (Frozen)

Document Version:
1.0

Status:
Released

That becomes your permanent baseline.

2. Add Guiding Principles

Before Module Readiness, I'd insert a short section.

Architecture Principles
Single Source of Truth

Product Platform is the only serialized product repository.

Append-Only Ledger

Inventory quantities are never overwritten.

Configuration over Hard Coding

Business rules are configuration-driven wherever possible.

Immutable Commercial Documents

Posted business documents are never edited.

Product Traceability First

Every serialized product must remain fully traceable throughout its lifecycle.

These principles explain why OCS One is designed the way it is.

3. Rename "Known Limitations"

I would rename:

Known Limitations

to

Known Operational Constraints

"Limitations" sounds like defects.

Most of those items are intentional design choices.

6. Strengthen the Recommendation

Instead of:

Recommendation:

APPROVE

I'd write:

Recommendation

The Engineering Team recommends approval of OCS One Factory Ready v1.0 for controlled commercial deployment.

Future development will continue under subsequent versioned releases without modifying the Factory Ready v1.0 baseline except through approved maintenance releases.

That's how commercial software is typically governed.

7. One sentence I'd definitely add

At the end:

This document establishes the Factory Ready v1.0 baseline. All future enhancements, modules, and architectural changes shall be versioned separately and must preserve backward compatibility with this baseline unless explicitly approved as a major platform revision.

I think that's an important governance statement.
