One design recommendation

Before implementing the PATCH endpoint, review the business rule:

Should a received lot ever be editable?

In many manufacturing execution systems, receiving records are immutable after acceptance. Instead of editing the original record, authorized users perform a Correction action that creates an audit entry while preserving the original data.

If OCS Oorja's factory requires full traceability, evaluate whether a Receive → Correct → Audit Trail workflow is more appropriate than unrestricted editing.

If business users confirm that edits are genuinely required before grading begins, then the PATCH endpoint is appropriate—but every change should be recorded in the audit history with:

Timestamp

New value
