# CTO Decision — Revised CW-03 Implementation Plan

After reviewing the implementation plan, I am approving CW-03 with one important change in sequencing.

The Unified Product Platform architecture has already been reviewed, approved, and frozen.

Therefore, it shall **not** be postponed until after Manufacturing certification.

**The Product Platform implementation becomes Phase 0 of CW-03.**

Manufacturing is the first workflow that creates a finished product. Therefore, the Product Platform must exist before Manufacturing certification begins.

This is **implementation of an approved architecture**, not architecture redesign.

---

# Revised CW-03 Roadmap

```

Phase 0
✓ Implement Product Platform

↓

Manufacturing Certification

↓

```

---

# Phase 0 – Product Platform Foundation

Before Manufacturing Orders implementation/certification begins, implement the frozen Product Platform v1.0 exactly as approved.

Implement only the approved architecture:

* Product Categories
* Product Workflows
* Product table
* Unified Product Serial model
* Product creation after QC PASS
* Product genealogy integration

Do **not** introduce additional concepts.

Do **not** redesign the architecture.

Do **not** extend the scope.

Simply implement the frozen Product Platform.

---

# Phase 1 – Manufacturing Orders

Refactor the existing Manufacturing Orders module to consume the Product Platform.

The manufacturing workflow itself remains unchanged.

The only architectural change is that QC PASS creates a Product record using the frozen Product Platform.

Manufacturing Orders shall no longer introduce its own finished-product identity.

---

# Phase 2 – QC Integration

Integrate the QC PASS gate with Product creation.

A Product shall exist only after QC PASS.

No Product shall exist before QC approval.

---

# Phase 3 – Manufacturing Certification

Execute the complete Manufacturing certification cycle:

* MAT-01
* MAT-02
* MAT-03
* MAT-04
* MAT-05
* MAT-06

against the Product-based Manufacturing module.

---

# Phase 4 – Freeze CW-03

Freeze the Manufacturing module after successful certification.

This becomes the official baseline for:

* Packing
* Dispatch
* Dealer
* Inventory
* Reports
* Director Dashboard

---

# Important Clarification

This work is **not** considered a platform redesign.

The Product Platform architecture has already been reviewed, approved, and frozen.

CW-03 only implements the approved architecture.

This avoids future refactoring of Manufacturing Orders and ensures that Packing, Dispatch, Dealer, Inventory, Reports, and future modules all consume a single Product identity from day one.

---

# First Deliverable

Revise the CW-03 implementation plan to reflect this sequencing.

Do not begin implementation until the revised plan has been reviewed and approved.
