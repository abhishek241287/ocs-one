Dealer Role (Factory Ready v1.0)

Dealer is not a factory user.

For this release:

Dealer can authenticate.
Dealer has access only to the Dealer Portal.
Dealer must not have access to factory modules.
Do not implement dealer-specific data ownership, customer scoping, inventory scoping, dispatch scoping, or service-request workflows in this sprint.

Those belong to Dealer Portal v2.

Explicitly Out of Scope

Do not implement:

Dealer → Dealer record mapping
Multi-tenant dealer filtering
Dealer inventory ownership
Dealer customer ownership
Dealer warranty ownership
Dealer service requests
Dealer approvals

These will be part of a future Dealer Portal enhancement after Factory Ready v1.0.

One change I'd make to the hierarchy

I would adjust one point slightly.

Instead of:

Director → Supervisor, Operator, Viewer

I'd allow:

Director → Director, Supervisor, Operator, Viewer, Dealer

with one restriction:

Only Owner can create or promote another Owner.

That avoids the awkward situation where an Owner must always create new Directors. Directors can manage their own organization, while Owner remains the highest governance role.

I think this keeps the RBAC clean, minimizes risk to your Factory Ready baseline, and leaves dealer self-service as a well-defined future project rather than expanding the scope of this release.
