# 1. Introduce Manufacturer as a Permanent Master

Please review whether Manufacturer should become an independent master entity.

Suggested examples:

* OCS
* Techfine
* Deye
* Growatt
* Voltronic
* MUST

A Product Model should reference Manufacturer.

Products should inherit Manufacturer from the Product Model.

Do not duplicate manufacturer information on every serialized product unless technically required.

Please evaluate whether this simplifies the architecture.

---

# 2. Simplify Product Serial Architecture

Instead of treating Manufacturer Serial Number and OCS Serial Number as equal concepts, evaluate a unified serial model.

Suggested design:

Official Product Serial

Serial Source

Serial Source values:

* OCS
* Manufacturer

Examples

Battery Pack

Official Product Serial = OCS250700001

Serial Source = OCS

---

Inbuilt Lithium Inverter

Official Product Serial = OCS250800001

Serial Source = OCS

---

Hybrid Inverter

Official Product Serial = TF-AB123456

Serial Source = Manufacturer

Downstream modules should work only with **Official Product Serial** and should never need to know whether it originated from OCS or the manufacturer.

Please evaluate whether this reduces system complexity.

---
