# OCS One – Sprint 7

## Battery Testing Laboratory & Quality Control

You are the Lead Manufacturing Software Engineer for OCS One.

Use the existing modules:

* Production Orders
* Battery Workspace
* Cell Grading
* Intelligent Cell Matching
* Assembly
* BMS Programming
* Charging & Formation
* Digital Battery Passport

Do not redesign existing modules.

Extend the Battery Workspace.

---

# Objective

After Charging & Formation, every battery must pass laboratory testing before QC approval.

Testing results become part of the permanent Digital Battery Passport.

A battery cannot move to QC until all mandatory tests pass.

---

# Module 1 – Test Equipment Management

Create a Test Equipment module.

Each tester stores:

* Equipment ID
* Equipment Type
* Manufacturer
* Model
* Serial Number
* Calibration Date
* Next Calibration Due
* Status (Available / Busy / Maintenance)

Supported equipment:

* Capacity Tester
* DC Load
* Protection Tester
* Internal Resistance Meter
* Thermal Camera

# Module 2 – Capacity Test

Create a Capacity Test stage.

Store:

* Test Equipment
* Operator
* Start Time
* End Time
* Rated Capacity (Ah)
* Measured Capacity (Ah)
* Capacity (%)
* Ambient Temperature
* Battery Temperature

Automatically calculate:

Pass / Fail

based on configurable limits.

# Rules

* A battery cannot be dispatched without QC approval.
* Test equipment in Maintenance cannot be selected.
* Calibration due dates must generate alerts.
* Every test result is immutable after QC approval.
* Every event is recorded in the audit log.
* Every report becomes part of the Digital Battery Passport.
