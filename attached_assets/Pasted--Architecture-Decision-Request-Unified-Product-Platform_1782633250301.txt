# Architecture Decision Request – Unified Product Platform

Before continuing CW-02, I want to freeze one important architecture decision because it will affect Manufacturing, Packing, Dispatch, Dealer, Inventory, Reports, Director Dashboard, and all future manufacturing modules.

This is an architecture activity only.

**Do not implement code.**

Review the current architecture first.

---

# Core Principle

Everything leaving the OCS factory becomes a **Product**.

Manufacturing workflows are different.

Product management is unified.

---

# Product Categories (Version 1.0)

There are only three product categories.

| Product Category         | Serial Number Rule                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Battery Pack             | OCS generates the serial number                                                         |
| Inbuilt Lithium Inverter | OCS generates the serial number if the imported unit has no manufacturer serial number  |
| Hybrid Inverter          | Techfine manufacturer serial number is reused as the official OCS product serial number |

No additional product categories are to be introduced in Version 1.0.

Future categories (ESS, EV Chargers, BMS, etc.) will be added later through the Product Framework.

---

# Important Business Rules

Hybrid Inverter and Inbuilt Lithium Inverter are completely different products.

## Inbuilt Lithium Inverter

Contains:

* Imported inverter PCB
* OCS Battery Pack (4S1P or 8S1P)
* BMS

The battery is permanently part of the product.

Manufacturing genealogy includes the battery.

OCS owns the product serial number.

---

## Hybrid Inverter

Techfine Hybrid Inverter.

Uses the Techfine manufacturer serial number as the official OCS product serial number.

No Battery Pack relationship exists during manufacturing.

Do not attach Battery genealogy.

External batteries belong to future Installation and Service modules.

They are **not** part of Manufacturing Version 1.0.
