# Objective

After QC Approval, every battery must be packed, assigned to a dispatch order, shipped to a dealer/customer, and remain traceable throughout its logistics lifecycle.

No battery can be dispatched unless it has passed QC.

# Rules

* Only QC Approved batteries can be packed.
* Packed batteries cannot return to production without a formal rework process.
* One battery belongs to only one dispatch order.
* Every logistics event is written to the audit log.
* Digital Passport updates automatically.
