CTO Approval — Certification Process Review

The Certification Process Review is approved.

The recommendations, templates, metrics, and lessons learned are accepted.

The Certification Framework is now frozen at Version 1.0.

No further changes to the Certification Framework shall be made during CW-03 through CW-08 except for:

* Critical certification defects
* Security vulnerabilities
* Architecture-breaking issues requiring immediate correction

All other ideas shall be recorded in the Certification Framework Backlog for a future framework release.

---

## New Engineering Directive

Beginning with CW-03, engineering effort shall be focused on manufacturing capability rather than certification process improvement.

The Certification Framework is now considered mature.

Future certification waves shall consume the framework rather than evolve it.

---

## Standing Rule

For every future certification wave:

* Reuse the standard MAT taxonomy.
* Reuse the standard documentation templates.
* Reuse the standard certification harness.
* Reuse the standard scorecards.
* Reuse the standard freeze package.

The objective is to reduce documentation effort while increasing manufacturing capability.

---

## Success Metric

From this point forward, project success shall primarily be measured by:

* Manufacturing capability delivered
* Platform adoption
* Product traceability
* Factory usability
* Reduction in certification effort per wave

rather than by creating additional framework features.

---

## CW-03 Authorization

CW-03 (Manufacturing Orders) is now authorized.

Before implementation begins:

1. Review the frozen Unified Product Platform architecture.
2. Review the frozen ECF architecture.
3. Review the frozen Product Platform architecture.
4. Produce a CW-03 implementation plan that consumes these frozen platforms without modifying them.

No implementation shall begin until the CW-03 implementation plan has been reviewed and approved.
