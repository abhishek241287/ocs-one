Now comes the most important milestone
M4 — GRN Integration

This is where I'd slow down and be very careful.

I would tell Replit not to rush M4.

Before writing code, I'd insist on verifying this exact flow:

        ▼

        ▼

        │
        ▼
Inventory Ledger
        │
        ▼

The linkage must remain automatic from end to end.

Another recommendation

On the Inventory screen, I would ask Replit to display additional provenance information.

For every inventory row, include:

Component Type

Then clicking the GRN number should open the original GRN.

That gives complete traceability with no duplicate storage.

One thing I would NOT do yet

I would not connect Production Orders to this new inventory until:

M4 is complete
M5 (Unified Inventory) is complete
M6 verification passes

Otherwise you'll be debugging procurement and manufacturing at the same time.

My only caution

The update mentions that the generic master form is becoming increasingly sophisticated (dynamic fields, conditional visibility, reconciliation hooks, etc.).

That's fine, but after M4 and M5 I would ask Replit to spend a little time ensuring those new generic capabilities don't unintentionally affect other master screens (Supplier, Dealer, BMS, Cable, etc.). A short regression pass across all master forms would be worthwhile before go-live.
